vm.sh open returns when the desktop of the VM fills the screen, ready for input.
Every vm.sh open starts the VM from its clean disk: what a run changes inside the VM is gone at the next open.
vm.sh close returns when the VM is off.
One VM is open at a time.
In arch_sway and omarchy_hyprland, Caps Lock is the Super key: Caps+Return opens a terminal, Caps+drag moves a window, Caps+T floats it on omarchy_hyprland.
A test on a VM gets /data/pilotio as the filesIOFolder argument of TestAgentTools.
