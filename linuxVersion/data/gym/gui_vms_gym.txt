A VM opens with
  /data/AgentsCoordination/linuxVersion/autoScripts/vm.sh open <vm>
which returns when the desktop of <vm> fills the screen, ready for input.
A VM closes with
  /data/AgentsCoordination/linuxVersion/autoScripts/vm.sh close <vm>
which returns when <vm> is off.
One VM is open at a time.
A test on a VM gets /data/pilotio as the filesIOFolder argument of TestAgentTools.
